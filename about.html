About

Luiz Paulo Alves Pinto is a seasoned mining engineer and PhD candidate at the Federal University of Rio Grande do Sul (UFRGS). His research focuses on applying machine learning to rock blasting optimisation. With a career spanning field operations and academic research, he brings a rare combination of technical depth and practical know‑how to every project.

From developing blast designs and managing quarries to analysing vibration and fragmentation, Luiz has worked with mining companies and infrastructure projects across Brazil. Today he offers remote consulting and training services to clients worldwide (except Brazil), delivering data‑driven insights and customised solutions.

  • M.Sc. in Mining Engineering – UFRGS (2019‑2022) — Mineral Sampling Optimisation.
  • B.Eng. Mining Engineering – UNIFAL/MG (2009‑2015).
