Predicting Vibration & Fragmentation Using Machine Learning

Ground vibrations and fragmentation outcomes are influenced by a complex combination of geological conditions, explosive characteristics, drill parameters and initiation sequences. Traditional empirical formulas — such as Kuz‑Ram — provide baseline predictions, but they often struggle to account for site‑specific variations.

Machine learning (ML) techniques offer a powerful alternative. By training models on historical blasting data, we can learn hidden patterns and interactions between variables. This allows us to forecast Peak Particle Velocity (PPV) and fragment size distributions with greater accuracy. Commonly used models include neural networks, support vector machines and ensemble methods.

In practice, ML‑based predictions help engineers make more informed decisions when selecting blast parameters, positioning holes and controlling charge distribution. The result is improved safety, reduced environmental impact and optimised downstream processes such as crushing and grinding.

Our consulting combines domain expertise with data science to build predictive models tailored to your site. We work with your data to calibrate and validate the models, ensuring that they provide actionable insights for future blasts.
