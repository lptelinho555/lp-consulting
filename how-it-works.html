How It Works

Our remote workflow makes it simple to engage with us wherever you are. Here’s how our process works from start to finish.

  • 1. Submit Your Data

    Provide us with the necessary technical data: drill plans, seismographic recordings, topographic files and any other relevant information. You can upload files directly via our contact form.

  • 2. Analysis & Optimisation

    We analyse your data using our proprietary digital tools and machine learning models. We evaluate blast designs, predict vibrations and fragmentation, and identify optimisation opportunities.

  • 3. Report & Delivery

    You receive a comprehensive technical report with recommendations, optimised blast parameters and predicted outcomes. Follow‑up consultation sessions are available to discuss results and implementation.
