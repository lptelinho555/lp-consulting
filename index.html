Remote drill and blast consulting for mines and quarries. I build machine learning models on your own blast data to predict fragmentation and ground vibration — so you cut downstream crushing costs and stay inside regulatory limits.

What this changes for your operation

  • Lower cost per tonne downstream

    Fragmentation drives the energy your crusher and mill consume — the largest power cost in the plant. A better size distribution at the muckpile means less work, and less cost, in everything that comes after it.

  • Vibration limits met without slowing down

    Peak particle velocity and airblast predicted before you fire, with reporting that holds up in front of regulators and neighbouring communities. Compliance without cutting your production rate.

  • Predictions from your data, not rules of thumb

    Models trained on your own blast records, geology and monitoring history. Scaled-distance charts treat every mine as the same mine — yours is not average.

  • Field practice with research depth

    Years designing blasts and managing quarries, combined with doctoral research at UFRGS on machine learning applied to rock blasting. Practical answers, current methods.
