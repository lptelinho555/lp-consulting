Why Choose Us

  • Proprietary Software Expertise

    Deep experience with proprietary and specialised digital tools for designing and planning open‑pit blasts. Our platforms integrate data from connected technologies across the drill and blast process to deliver real‑time collection, management and analysis. By capturing data at every stage and applying machine learning and regression analysis, these tools generate precise design inputs and can predict vibrations and fragmentation outcomes.

  • Machine Learning

    We apply advanced machine learning models to optimise blast parameters, predict ground vibrations and improve fragment size distribution.

  • Field & Academic Experience

    Years of hands‑on fieldwork combined with cutting‑edge academic research ensure practical yet innovative solutions to your blasting challenges.

  • Global Remote Service

    Consulting and training services are delivered remotely, accessible from anywhere in the world (except Brazil). We tailor our approach to your time zone and operational needs.
