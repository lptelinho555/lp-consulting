Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-30

Who is responsible

This website is operated by Luiz Paulo Alves Pinto, an independent consultant providing remote drill and blast services. For any question about your data, write to luizpauloalvespinto@yahoo.com.br.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us through the contact form:

  • Your name and email address (required to reply)
  • Your company name (optional)
  • The project description you write
  • Any technical file you choose to attach

We do not use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies. The site sets no cookies of any kind.

Why we use it and on what basis

Your data is used solely to answer your enquiry and to prepare a proposal if you request one. The legal basis is your consent, given when you submit the form, and our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries. We never sell or share your data for marketing.

Third parties involved

Two services process data on our behalf:

  • FormSubmit — receives the contact form submission and forwards it to our email. Their servers are outside the EU.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website and records standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security purposes.

Sending confidential or commercially sensitive technical data through the form is at your discretion. If your material is under NDA, please contact us by email first so we can agree on a secure transfer method.

How long we keep it

Enquiry emails are kept for as long as the commercial relationship is active, and up to 24 months afterwards, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Write to luizpauloalvespinto@yahoo.com.br and we will respond within 30 days. If you are in the EU or UK you may also complain to your national data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published on this page with a new date.
