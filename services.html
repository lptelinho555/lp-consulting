Services

Each engagement starts from a problem you already have — oversize at the crusher, vibration complaints, cost per tonne that will not come down — and works back to the blast.

  • Fragmentation optimisation

    Reduce oversize and fines, and cut the energy your crusher and mill consume. I analyse your drill pattern, explosive selection, timing and geology, then redesign for the size distribution your plant actually wants.

  • Vibration and airblast compliance

    Predict peak particle velocity and airblast before you fire, interpret seismograph records, and produce reports that satisfy regulators and neighbouring communities — without cutting your production rate.

  • Predictive models built on your data

    Custom machine learning and regression models trained on your blast history, geology and monitoring records, so predictions reflect your site rather than a generic scaled-distance curve.

  • Blast design review

    An independent read of your current designs: drill pattern, powder factor, initiation sequence and explosive selection, with specific changes and the reasoning behind each one.

  • Team training

    Your engineers and blasters learn the design principles, software and seismography methods, so the capability stays with your operation after the project ends.

How pricing works

Every operation is different, so every engagement is scoped individually rather than sold from a price list.

We start with a free diagnostic call. You describe the problem, I tell you whether I can help and what it would take. If it is a fit, you receive a written scope with a fixed price and clear deliverables before any commitment.

Ongoing monthly support is available for operations that want continuous optimisation rather than a one-off project.
