Services

We provide remote technical services for drilling and blasting operations around the world. Each service is tailored to your project goals and challenges.

  • Blast Design Analysis & Optimisation

    Comprehensive review and optimisation of your existing blast designs using our proprietary drill & blast software and specialised digital tools. We analyse drill patterns, explosive selection and firing sequences to enhance efficiency and reduce costs.

  • Seismic Analysis & Compliance Reports

    Interpretation of seismographic data, Peak Particle Velocity (PPV) and airblast analysis with detailed reports that meet international standards and regulations.

  • ML‑based Vibration & Fragmentation Prediction

    Development of predictive models using machine learning to forecast ground vibration and fragmentation outcomes, supporting decision‑making and compliance.

  • Remote Training

    Personalised training sessions covering our proprietary software, blast design principles and seismography techniques. Delivered via secure video conferencing with hands‑on demonstrations.

  • Technical Consulting & Problem‑Solving

    Expert advice for complex blasting challenges, including troubleshooting, design adjustments and strategic planning to improve safety and performance.

Pricing

My consulting services are competitively priced to deliver maximum value while remaining accessible to mining operations of all sizes.

  • Hourly remote technical consulting: starting at $50 USD per hour, with project‑based pricing for defined deliverables.
  • Blast design analysis using specialised software: typically ranges from $200 to $400.
  • Seismic monitoring & compliance reports: from $250 to $450.
  • Custom machine learning models for vibration or fragmentation: from $350 to $600.
  • Training sessions: $60 to $90 per hour, with discounted packages available for ongoing monthly support.

Initial consultations are always free of charge. For a detailed quote tailored to your specific needs, please contact us directly.
